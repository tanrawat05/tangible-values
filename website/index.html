The silver Rupee is a classic example of sovereign coinage from the Princely State of Malerkotla. Issued under the authority of Nawab Amir Khan, this piece represents the pinnacle of local numismatic tradition. The 1862 silver ½ Rupee represents the transition to unified colonial coinage under the British Crown. Introduced after the East India Company's rule ended, it features Queen Victoria's crowned profile, symbolising imperial power and centralised governance. Struck in fine silver, the coin reflects a shift towards standardisation, reinforcing Britain's economic control while circulating widely across the Indian subcontinent as a tool of both trade and political presence.

1862–

Silver ½ Rupee

The British India ½ Rupee (1862), issued under Queen Victoria, marks the formalisation of colonial coinage after the 1857 uprising. Featuring a crowned portrait and English inscriptions, it reflects imperial authority, standardised currency, and expanding British control over India's economic systems.

Birth of
a Nation

The 1947 Quarter Rupee represents a defining shift in India's monetary and political history. Issued at the moment of independence, it replaces imperial imagery with national symbolism, most notably the tiger, embodying resilience and power. The use of multiple scripts reflects linguistic diversity and inclusivity, while its widespread circulation established it as a symbol of sovereignty, economic continuity, and the beginning of a self-governed future.

Independent India

1947 AD

Quarter Rupee

The India Quarter Rupee (1947) marks the nation's transition to independence following the end of British colonial rule. Featuring the iconic tiger motif and multilingual inscriptions, it symbolises strength, unity, and the emergence of a new national identity during a pivotal historical moment.

The ₹10 bimetallic coin is a defining example of modern Indian currency design. Combining a brass outer ring with a nickel-brass core, it symbolises strength and stability. Widely circulated, the coin carries the official rupee symbol and denomination, representing India's economic progress, unity in diversity, and transition into a globally connected financial system.
